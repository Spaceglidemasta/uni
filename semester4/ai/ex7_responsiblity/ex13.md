## 1 AI Resume Screening System
### Pros:
1. Less work / cost for HR / the stakeholder
2. Filtering out trash / unreasonably low effort applications
3. Faster evaluation of applications

### Cons:
1. Giving very personal data to an AI (NLP + ML)
2. Trusting an AI with handeling applications -> "who is at fault, when the AI fails?"
3. One more job replaced by AI

### How to mitigate Risks: 
- Only let the AI filter out very clear cases (very bad / good applications) and let the rest be evaluated by a human
- Obfurscate Personal Data before feeding CV's to the AI
- Confirm rejection of applications by human oversight

### EU AI Act risk category estimation
High Risk (Limited Risk with human oversight)
